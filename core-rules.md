## Action Roll

1. Roll two six-sided dice and sum the pips.
2. Add any additional pips granted by abilities.

| Pips       | Outcome           |
| ---------- | ----------------- |
| 6 or less  | Failure           |
| 7 to 9     | Success at a cost |
| 10 or more | Full success      |

### Advantage & Disadvantage

- If you have **advantage** on an Action Roll, roll three dice and take the highest two.
- If you have **disadvantage**, roll three dice and take the lowest two.
- If you have **both**, make the roll as if you had neither.
