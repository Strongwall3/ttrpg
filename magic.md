In this game, **Practitioners** of magic create supernatural effects by combining the **domains** and **techniques** that they know.

A **domain** is a part of reality on which magic can act. Examples include Fire, Blood, and Dream.

A **technique** is what the spell does to the domain. Examples include Conjuring, Dominating, and Warding.

## Aspects of a spell

Each spell has the following aspects:

- Domain
- Technique
- Range
- Area
- Duration

### Domains

Domains are conceptual, not fundamental, and in theory anything could be a domain. The domains presented here are the most common, widely-practised, and versatile.

### Duration

The duration of a spell refers to the amount of time it's magical effect exists for.

- Instant
- Sixty breaths (a few minutes)
- Until sunrise
- Until death of the caster
- Forever

**Instant.** Instant spells have no lasting effect. Example spells include lighting a campfire, changing the weather, and banishing a shade. Nothing supernatural occurs after the spell is cast, only the natural consequences of the change.

**Brief.** Brief spells last for a duration of sixty breaths (roughly three to five minutes).

**Lasting.**

**Eternal.** An eternal spell is a great feat of sorcery, requiring the practitioner to weave it in a self-sustaining way. The magical effects of this spell will persist indefinitely, unless broken by another spell.

### Group casting

Multiple practitioners can work together on the same spell. When doing so, domains and techniques can be selected from any of the participants' repertoires. However, the spell takes a minute to cast and all participants will suffer any negative side effects.
